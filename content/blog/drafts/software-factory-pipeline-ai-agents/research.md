## Facts that are proposals, stated as proposals in the article

- The five roles and their write scopes (Orchestrator, Explorer, Implementer, Reviewer, Integrator).
- One automatic correction per failure type, then human escalation.
- The resume rule (task record stores last step and last evidence SHA; resume re-runs evidence).
- The lease rule (short-lived claim that rejects duplicate task pickup).
- Draft PR creation after approval; human-only merge, publish, deploy.

None of these is implemented anywhere in this repository, and the article marks each of them proposed.
