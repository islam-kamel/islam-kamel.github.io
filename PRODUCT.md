# Product

## Purpose

A personal website for Islam Kamel: a clear introduction, a factual overview of technical interests and education, and a home for readable technical writing.

## Audience and actions

Visitors should be able to understand what the site covers, browse technical articles, and contact Islam. Contact is personal; do not imply a recruiting, freelance, or contract funnel. The primary contact wording is “Say hello”.

## Experience priorities

- Make the homepage identity and article paths immediately legible.
- Give the homepage, blog index, and articles one coherent retro editorial visual language.
- Keep article reading comfortable and accessible; decorative styling must not compete with prose or code.
- Preserve factual content and existing route behavior. Do not invent projects, credentials, metrics, or personal claims.

## Boundaries

This is a personal site, not a product dashboard or agency portfolio. Do not add fake charts, testimonials, recruitment copy, service packages, or decorative interactions without a clear purpose.

Use natural labels such as About, What I build, Education, Writing, and Say hello. Avoid pseudo-code, bracketed section names, slash-heavy separators, all-caps jargon, invented file paths, and console/system language. Keep credentials factual and name their issuing institutions; do not call them “verified” or “accreditations” without evidence. The owner rejected schematic computer artwork; the approved hero art is the abstract pink-cloud and halftone collage at `public/retro-cloud-collage.webp`.
